Like Double and Triple Zigzags, usually Double and Triple
Combinations will channel within well defined parallel lines, until the last
corrective phase is nearing completion. Mentioned earlier, most Double
and Triple Combinations conclude with a Triangle. As you know from
previous discussions, Triangles create problems with Channeling,
‘Therefore, the last few segments of the Triangle, which will conclude the
last corrective phase of the Double or Triple Combination, will provide at
least one (if not two or more) ‘false’ breaks of the base parallel line before
finally completing, See Figure 12-20 for some examples.
