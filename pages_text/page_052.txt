Only one figure per time period should be employed so it is possible to
decide how each figure changes (if at all) the current expectations and
possibilities of the market. This does not mean only one figure per day
should be used for your analysis, simply that a single figure per time unit
(whatever that time unit is - one day, week, hour, etc.) is optimal. This
allows you to arrive at a definitive conclusion on current market conditions
and future expectations. A price band (the bar line on a bar chart) does not
