If you start a count from a point on a chart which is not the termination
of an Elliott pattern, your analysis could run astray for long petiods of
time. Eventually you should be able to recognize the mistake and make the
appropriate adjustments. Unfortunately, by the time a mistake is detected,
the market move could be nearing completion or you may have taken
several losses due to trades initiated based on inaccurate assumptions. All
this is, of course, due to starting a count from a “visually” important top or
bottom which is not the termination of an Elliott pattern.
