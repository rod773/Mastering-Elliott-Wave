Double & Triple Combinations (which start with Flats)

Because there are many variational possibilities with such patterns, itis
difficult to create any Channeling process which will work for all
possibilities. The baseline, composed of b-wave termination points, will
always be more reliable than the trendline composed of a-wave
termination points. The best approach is to continue using the b-wave's
baseline as the important channel and realize that a-wave termination
points may not create a very clean trendline. In Diagram A of Figure 12-
22, the trendline is satisfactory, but in Diagram B it does not perform its
function as well.
