One of the last, critical considerations of an interpretation’s validity
involves the incorporation of Logic into the market action. This is an area
of the Wave Theory which is completely overlooked by the majority. The
Logic rules are the product of exhaustive market research and observation
by the author, from an Elliott Wave standpoint, for over a half-decade. The
essence of the Logic Rulles: Post-pattern market action must adhere to
specific types of behavior which is predicated upon the pattern just
completed.

For example: after completing a Terminal Impulse pattern, the reaction
should be quite violent and should retrace all of the Terminal pattem
within less than 50% of the time consumed by the Terminal Impulse. Lack
of obedience to this Rule indicates a significant flaw with the current
interpretation. If you thought you had uncovered a Terminal pattern in a
market, but post-Terminal behavior did not react as described, a Terminal
Impulse pattern never existed in the configuration predicted,
