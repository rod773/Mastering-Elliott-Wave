What is the Elliott Wave Theory?

Price Patterns of Psychology

From an Elliott Wave perspective, the plotted price activity of a market
is the graphical representation of crowd psychology. The Wave Theory
describes how local plotted data relates to surrounding data, how data
should behave under a multitude of circumstances, when and how
psychological trends begin and end, how one psychological environment
‘mandates the unfolding of another and what general shape the price action
should exhibit upon completion. In other words, the Elliott Wave Theory
