b. Irregular

‘The Irregular Triangle is characterized by the b-wave of the pattern,
which must be longer (pricewise) than wave-a. After wave-b, all waves are
continuously smaller until the Triangle is over.

Wave-a

With few exceptions, the a-wave should be smaller in time than the b-
wave, The b-wave should not be more than 161.8% of wave-a (price-
wise). Wave-c must move back into the price "zone" of wave-b enough so
that the shorter e-wave can also finish in the price zone of wave-b. The a-
wave can be any corrective pattern except a Triple Zigzag or an Elongated
Flat. To avoid confusion, I will once again mention that wave a,b,c, or d of
a Triangle can never, itself, be a Triangle. The e-wave is the only segment
of a Triangle allowed to form into a smaller Triangle.
