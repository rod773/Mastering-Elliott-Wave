Internal

Internal relationships are characterized by the comparison of the price
length of one wave to another with no concer over the price level each
wave began or ended (see Figure 12-25). These price ranges will usually
partially overlap. This means part of one move will be in the same price
level zone as the other. Relationships in Impulse patterns, with a few
exceptions, are almost exclusively Internal. Depending on the
circumstances, relationships in corrections can fall into both camps. Below
is a complete listing of the most common (and some of the less common)
‘ways Internal relationships occur in Impulse patterns.
