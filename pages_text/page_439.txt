Impulse Identification with Channeling

With an understanding of the different ways the various Impulse
pattems channel, you can frequently use Channeling to decipher which
wave of the pattem is going to be the Extension, The earlier you decide
which wave is the Extension the better you can trade the trend. The
explanations and illustrations to follow will provide you with a clear idea
cof how each Impulse variation Channels and behaves.

Ast Wave Extension

When the first wave Extends in a sequence, the channeling of the
pattern should resemble the channeling of a Terminal move (Figure 12-
12), The Sth wave should not touch the upper trendline. It will usualy stay
below it, but on occasion, if wave-2 is very large it might exceed the
upper trendline.
