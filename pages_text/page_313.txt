Construction of Complex Multiwaves

‘The discussion on Complex Multiwaves is basically the same as
Complex Polywaves. The only difference is Complex Multiwaves are
made from a collection of Multiwaves, not a collection of Polywaves. If
you witness a Corrective Multiwave which is retraced 161.8% or more by
the next correction, OR is retraced less than 61.8% by the next corrective
phase, then a Complex pattem is forming. Apply the same Rules and
principles as those discussed in the Complex Polywave section.
