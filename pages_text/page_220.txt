Triangles (3-3-3-3-3)

Some of the most difficult patterns to work with under the Wave
Theory are variations ofTriangles:They have no specific time limits for
completion. Absolute certainty as to the direction of postTriangular action
is often impossible. One of their redeeming qualities is that after they
complete, they provide a significant amount of information on current
market position and offer numerous clues on how post-triangular price
action should behave for extended periods of time.

Despite their difficulties, Triangles are some of the most common
Elliott pattems; therefore, a thorough understanding of them is essential.
Learning to identify Triangles early in their formation can save you from
many hours of from frustration and from unnecessary trading. losses
(specially in Options). Following are the most important Rules and
characteristics of Triangular formations. The majority of these Rules
(which are essential) are presented here for the first time, so pay close
attention.
