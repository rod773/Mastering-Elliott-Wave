Which patterns are susceptible?

‘The most susceptible pattems to missing waves are Complex
Corrective Polywaves (corrections that involve x-waves). Virtually
without exceptions, it is the x-wave which is "missing" in one of these
patterns (see “emulation” for a more detailed description). This is due to
the fact that x-waves are almost always the smallest corrective waves of
stich patterns, so they are the first to disappear as the time element of the
Correction is reduced, Much less frequently, Impulsive poly wave patterns
which have a 1 st or Sth wave Extension can “miss” their smaller
corrective phase. In a Sth wave Extension that would be wave2 (as in
