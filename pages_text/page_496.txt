Expansion of Possibilities

This is a completely new conceptual discovery by the author which, for
the first time, helps you overcome the problem of altemate counts. When
you are working with price action, there are time periods when the Eliott
pattern forming is clear and no reliable alternatives to the interpretation
exist. At other times, numerous possibilities exist which can leave many
analysts in a quandary

As remarkable as it may sound, this state of confusion can be exploited
‘© aid you in deciphering the market’s position; if not specifically, atleast
generally. This involves a process I call "Reverse Logic.” Plainly, the rule
states: When more than one, perfectly acceptable wave interpretation
is possible from the same data series, the market must be near the
center of a Corrective or Impulsive pattern. The more possibilities
that exist the closer the of a large Elliott
formation (wave-b of wave-b; wave-3 of wave-3 or wave-x of a Non-
Standard Complex Correction).
