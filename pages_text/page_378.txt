Wave-4

If the Sth wave extends, wave-4 should be more complex, time-
consuming, and possibly of one greater complexity level than wave-2. If
wwave-1 extends, wave-4 should be simpler in price, time and structure than
wave-2. If the fifth wave extends, wave-4 should retrace a greater
percentage of wave3 than wave-2 does of wave-1. Frequently wave-4 will
Tetrace as much as 50-61.8% of wave-3 when the Sth wave is the
extension. Ifthe fifth wave fails in the Impulse sequence, the fourth wave
should be the most complex pattern and should retrace more than 38.2% of
wwave-3 (as much as 61.8% is allowable).

Wave-5 Extended

Wave-5 should be atleast equal to the price distance from the
beginning of wave-1 (point "O") through the end of wave-3 added to the
end of wave-4, The maximum length of the Sth extension should not
exceed 261.8% of the length from "0" to the end of wave-3 added to the
end of wave-3. When considering waves-2 and 4, wave-4 should take the
greatest amount of price’time and have the most complex structure. Wave-
1 should progress at the sharpest angle with wave-3 following closely
behind and wave-5 possessing the slowest rate of acceleration. A Sth wave
extension cannot be completely retraced unless it is the c-wave of a
correction or is the end of a larger Sth wave Extension,
