Elongated

An Elongated pattern adheres to all of the general specifications of a
Flat pattern with the following additional criteria:

1. Wave-c must be more than 138.2% of wave-b (preferably, more than
161.8%); see Figure 11-13.

2, Wave-a and wave-b should be similar in price and/or time (one of
the few times that Alternation have a major impact on
these two adjacent waves), while wave-c consumes a significant
amount more of both (refer to Figure 11-14).
