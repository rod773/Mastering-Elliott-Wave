‘When first trying to analyze a market's price action, You may be
tempted to start with long-term price charts. In doing this, you could easily
risinterpret the position of the market due to a common mistake; that of
starting the count from a major high or low (see Figure 8-21). To start an
interpretation from the improper point makes it very difficult to correctly
predict what will take place in the future, Believe it or not, most large
Elliott pattems do not finish at the highest or lowest price on a chart. This
is an inherent part of the Wave Theory which is frequently misunderstood
and is essentially unique to Elliott. I say it is unique since most techniques
look at the highest high or lowest low as the important price or time
‘measuring point for further calculations. Often, with Elliott, points below
the high or above the low ate mote important for specific calculations than
the actual high or low. The concept of a pattern finishing after a high or
low is one of the significant reasons many Ellioticians experience
difficulty applying Eliot to longer time periods.
