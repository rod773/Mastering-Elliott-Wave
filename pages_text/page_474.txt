Wave-c in Triangles

‘The c-wave of a Triangle usually relates to wave-a by 61.8%, but do
not eliminate a Triangle count just because that parameter is not met (Fig.
12-35). If wave-b is bigger than wave-a, wave-c might be 61.8% of wave-
b (Fig. 12-36). Remember, Triangles are probably the most common,
flexible, varied, and annoying of all Elliott patterns. As long as the market
experiences an obvious contraction period composed of five separate
segments and there are at least two Fibonacci relationships that occur
between the various segments, you have a likely candidate for a Triangle
(ee Chapter 5 for additional Rules)

Wave-d
Wave-d only occurs in Triangles. It will normally relate to wave-b by
61.8%. Wave-d could relate to virtually any wave in the Triangle by
61.8% or 38.2%.

Wave-e
‘Wave-e will usually relate to wave-d by 61.8% oF 38.2%. It could also
relate to the larger waves by 38.2%. If it relates to wave-a it would
probably be because the b-wave were larger than wave-a
